Biomass–fire test

Climate similarity test
Areas with different fire frequencies are climatically similar within each ecosystem — ensuring that observed biomass differences across fire classes reflect fire regime, not underlying climate gradients.
What this means: Only ecosystem types passing both tests are included. For these, fire frequency can serve as a credible proxy for ecosystem condition.
